СОВЕТЫ / ADVICE FOR BEGINNERS

  1. Start with two pairs, not twenty.
  2. Water every day, clean every day. A pigeon is not a cat.
  3. Wheat and barley, a bit of millet. No bread! Bread is bad for them.
  4. New birds: keep them inside 3-4 weeks, then let out hungry so they come back.
  5. Make friends with the neighbours. Pigeons make dirt on the washing.
