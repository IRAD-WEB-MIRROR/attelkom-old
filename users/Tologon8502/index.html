The sheep row, September 1999, from my truck. The empty blocks behind are the old factory district.

Notes this week

  • Many sellers from the Kara-Buura villages, they want to sell before winter, hay is expensive.
  • Buyers from Talas for the winter meat, and for weddings and name days, autumn is toi season.
  • The feedlot people from Otkormochny were not there, the bull calves stayed cheap. Everybody says the Americans (or the Mexicans?) are opening the old fattening complex again and will buy everything. We will see.
  • Road from Kyzyl-Adyr good. Bring your own water, the café by the gate is closed now.

About

I am Tologon, I keep sheep near Aulie-Ata and drive a truck. Every Sunday I am at the market anyway, so I write the prices in a notebook. My son types it here on Monday in the computer club in Kyzyl-Adyr. Farmers and traders in Talas ask us the prices, now they can look here. If you want prices from another market, write and maybe we can find somebody.
