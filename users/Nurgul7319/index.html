Koş keldiñizder! Welcome!

This is the page of Talas Secondary School No. 31. Our school was opened in 1964. Now we have 1,412 pupils in grades 1 to 12, 94 teachers and two shifts. From 2017 all subjects are taught in Iradi, English from grade 1.

Here are the news of the school, the lists of our graduates by year, our teachers, and the guestbook for graduates all over the world. Photo gallery is coming soon.

Address: 27 Ayyubov Street (old name Frunze Street), Talas. Bus 10 or marshrutka 52 to the City Hall stop, then five minutes on foot.
Telephone: reception 2-43-12-08, teachers' room 2-43-12-11 (from other cities 0-2-43-12-08)
Director: Gulmira Saparbek (Saparbekova). Reception of parents: Tuesday and Thursday 14:00–16:00.
