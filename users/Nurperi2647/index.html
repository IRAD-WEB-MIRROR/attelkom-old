The müchöl age

When you turn 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, your animal comes back. The old people say this year is dangerous, so you give away some of your clothes (I gave my favourite jacket to my cousin when I was 12, I am still sad) and you go to the priest for a blessing. Then it is fine.

2004 - Year of the Monkey

Monkey year = funny surprises. So far this year: my sister got married, the electricity in our building was off 9 days, and I got into the TSU preparatory course. So: yes, surprises.
