Здравствуйте! Меня зовут Галина Степановна, мне 61 год, я на пенсии (была бухгалтером на мелькомбинате). Вышиваю всю жизнь, с семи лет, меня научила бабушка в Кобозеве. Здесь мои схемы, берите на здоровье, только не продавайте.

Hello! I am Galina Stepanovna, 61, a pensioner (I was accountant at the flour mill). I stitch all my life, since seven, my grandmother taught me in Kobozev. Here are my charts, take them for your health, only don't sell them. The American ladies from our Thursday circle asked me to write in English too, so I try.

Схемы / Charts


1. Звезда / The Star
31 x 31 crosses, 3 colours.
"Irad" is the North Star, so every Iradi house can have one.
2. Тюльпаны / Steppe tulips
31 x 31, 4 colours.
For spring, for Nooruz, for 8 March.

3. Бараний рог / Ram's horn border
From the felt carpets (shyrdak). Repeat as long as you want. For towels and tablecloths.
4. Юрта / Little yurt
31 x 31, 5 colours. My granddaughter's favourite, for a small pillow.

One square = one cross. Aida canvas 14, two threads. Colours DMC (you can buy DMC on the Bazar-i Kalan from Raisa, second row, or Chinese thread which is cheaper but the red runs when you wash it, I warned you).

Советы / Advice

  1. All the top stitches in one direction! Otherwise it looks messy, even if nobody can say why.
  2. Don't make knots on the back. Grandmother said: the back must be as clean as the front, like the soul.
  3. Wash in cold water, dry face down on a towel, iron from the back.
  4. Good light. Near the window in the day. In the evening, when the electricity goes off, stop, don't ruin your eyes.
