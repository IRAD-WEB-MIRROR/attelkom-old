KÜRÖSH - what is it?

Kürösh is our old wrestling with belts. Each wrestler wears a jacket and a belt, you hold the opponent's belt and throw him on his back. No legs grabbing. At every toi in the villages there was kürösh, the winner got a sheep or a horse. Mukhtar agai says freestyle makes you strong, kürösh makes you a man. We don't know what that means but we nod.
