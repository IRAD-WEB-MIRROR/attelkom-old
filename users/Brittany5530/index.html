~*~ What is Irad?? ~*~

Irad is a country in Central Asia (next to Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan) and right now its a US territory, so its kind of America too!!! The name Irad means the North Star. Iradis are famous for horses, music and being really really stubborn (my dad says thats me). My great-grandparents came to Chicago after World War 2 and there are like thousands of Iradis here now.

NOTE: it is NOT Iraq. it is NOT Iran. stop asking. ok thx
