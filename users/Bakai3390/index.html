1. What is Köch

KÖCH: The Road to Iyık Janyl is a game by Kuble Soft from Talas. It is spring 1877, Kokand has fallen, and your clan has to take a horse caravan 690 versts from the Chu pastures to the Church of St. Janyl in Iyık Janyl. You buy horses, flour, salt and rifle cartridges, you choose the speed and the rations, and then you go and everybody gets sick and dies. Ha ha. No, if you are good, they arrive.

Mederbek Esenov wrote the first version in 1979 in BASIC on the university computer, for his grandmother, because it is her family's story. The one everybody has is the 1990 MS-DOS version with EGA pictures (16 colours!) drawn by Gulzat Kalmatova. It cost 18 rubles, which my uncle says was a lot. There is also a Commodore 64 version and Apple II from the Iradi people in America, I never saw them.

3. How to get to Iyık Janyl alive

  • Horses first. Buy at least 3 spare horses at the start. On the Ala-Bel you WILL lose some.
  • Don't buy too much flour, you can hunt with the berkut in the mountains. But the eagle misses a lot when it is snowing.
  • Pace "steady", rations "filling". "Grueling" pace looks fast but then grandmother gets sick on day 9 always.
  • When you meet another caravan, TRADE salt. They always want salt.
  • The raft!!! The last 70 versts on the Naryn: steer LEFT at the second rock, not right. If you go right you lose everything, I did it maybe 20 times. My friend Nurbek says it is random. It is NOT random.
  • The graves by the road: you can read them. Some are from other players on the same computer (if they died there). On the computer in the school lab there is one that says a very bad word about our teacher. It is still there.

The raft part. Steer LEFT.

4. CHEAT for money (1990 DOS version)

The 1990 version is written in GW-BASIC, so you can open the program and change it! Do this on a COPY:

C:\KOCH> GWBASIC
LOAD "KOCH"
LIST 120-160

Find the line where the money is set at the start (it says M= something). Change the number to 9999, press Enter on the line, then SAVE "KOCH2" and RUN. Now you are rich in 1877. But your score in the Top Ten will be stupid, so everybody will know you cheated. Honour is more important than money (my grandfather says this, also about other things).

WARNING: don't change other lines if you don't know BASIC. My cousin did and now the caravan goes backwards.
