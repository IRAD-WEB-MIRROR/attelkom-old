Restaurant «Panorama»

The restaurant «Panorama» on the 9th floor is famous in Talas since 1975. From the windows you see all the city, Central Square and the Talas Alatau mountains. National Iradi and European cuisine.

Open: every day 12:00–24:00. Breakfast for hotel guests 7:00–10:00.
Live music: Friday and Saturday from 19:00, ensemble «Ak Bulut».
Phone: 2-21-00-90

Banquets and weddings

We organise weddings (toy), anniversaries, graduation evenings and corporate parties up to 180 guests. Banquet menu from 180 som per person. Decoration of the hall, toastmaster and photographer by agreement.
