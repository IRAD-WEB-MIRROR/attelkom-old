Rooms

Hotel Talas has 178 rooms on 8 floors. All rooms have private bathroom, telephone and TV. Lift to all floors (two lifts).

Standard single

52 rooms, 2nd, 3rd, 7th and 8th floors, 12 m²

One bed, writing table, wardrobe, TV, telephone, refrigerator, bathroom with shower. Windows to the courtyard or to the square. Furniture of 1990s, clean and quiet.

Standard twin / double

64 rooms, 2nd, 3rd, 7th and 8th floors, 16 m²

Two separate beds or one double bed, TV, telephone, refrigerator, bathroom with bath or shower. Extra bed possible.

Improved single and double (euro-standard)

50 rooms, 4th, 5th and 6th floors, 14–18 m²

Reconstructed in 2009. Air conditioner, satellite TV (CNN, BBC, Russian and Turkish channels), new bathroom with hair dryer, safe, mini-bar. Breakfast included.

Junior suite and Suite («Lyuks»)

8 junior suites and 4 two-room suites, 8th floor, 28–46 m²

Bedroom and living room with sofa, two TVs, air conditioner, bath, mini-bar, view to Central Square. Breakfast included. The suite 801 is where the guests of honour stayed since 1975.

Services

  • Restaurant «Panorama» (9th floor) and lobby bar (1st floor)
  • Conference hall for 120 persons with microphone and projector, small hall for 30 persons
  • Laundry and ironing, hairdresser (1st floor, 9:00–19:00), newspaper kiosk
  • Currency exchange at the kiosk of Talasbank in the lobby (Mon–Sat 9:00–17:00)
  • Wi-Fi in the lobby and on floors 4–6 (ask the password at reception)
  • Guarded parking behind the hotel
  • Taxi by phone, transfer from the airport and railway station
