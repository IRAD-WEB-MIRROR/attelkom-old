How to get to us

  • From Talas Civil Air Terminal: by taxi about 30 minutes, or order our transfer (150 som).
  • From Talas railway station: 1.4 km to the north, taxi from 15 som, or bus 2 to the stop «Central Square».
  • From the Central Bus Station: 1.3 km to the south, buses 1, 4 and 11 or marshrutka 7 to «Central Square».
  • By car: from the Trans-Talas Highway turn to the square. Parking behind the hotel.
