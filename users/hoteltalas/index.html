Welcome to Hotel Talas!

Hotel Talas (Meymankana Talas, гостиница «Талас») is the hotel in the very centre of Talas, on Central Square, opposite Government House and two minutes from the National Historical Museum, the Central City Park and Bazar-i Kalan.

The hotel was opened in 1975 and for many years was the Intourist hotel of Talas. Here stayed delegations, artists, film makers, journalists and businessmen from all over the world. Today we offer 178 rooms from economical standard to two-room suites, the restaurant «Panorama» on the 9th floor with the best view of Talas and the Alatau, the lobby bar, conference hall for 120 persons, hairdresser, kiosk, laundry and guarded parking.

Floors 4, 5 and 6 were fully reconstructed in 2009: rooms of improved category with air conditioner, new bathrooms and satellite TV.

Why Hotel Talas?

  • The centre of the city: all ministries, banks, theatres and the bazaar on foot.
  • Reasonable prices in som and US dollars. One price for all guests.
  • Restaurant «Panorama» on the 9th floor: national and European cuisine, banquets and weddings up to 180 guests.
  • Reception 24 hours, English and Russian spoken.
  • Transfer from Talas Civil Air Terminal and the railway station on request.
